Chapter 01 — 轻 / Weightless

Lighterthan air.

Every girl is handed something fragile early on: a balloon, a bubble, a promise. The first act of heroism is neither letting go nor gripping so hard it breaks.

每个女孩很早就会被交到手里一样易碎的东西：气球、泡泡、一个承诺。英雄主义的第一步，是既不松手，也不攥碎。

Float first.
Fly later.

— Rule zero, age eleven

R1

Hold loosely松而不脱

A string, not a fist.

R2

Rise slowly缓慢上升

Altitude is earned in centimetres.

R3

Shine when struck遇光则亮

Let the light show you your own edges.
